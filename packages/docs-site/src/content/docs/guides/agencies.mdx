---
title: Agencies and client sites
description: Run many client websites from one Typeroll Organization, publish each to the right Cloudflare account, give clients access to only their site and hand work over cleanly.
---

An agency usually builds and maintains many websites for different clients.
In Typeroll, an **Organization** owns sites and the publishing connections
they share. This guide shows how to set up client work so that each client
sees only their own site, each site publishes to the right Cloudflare account,
and your AI agents can work across all of them.

## Decide who owns each site

Settle this before building, because Typeroll has no transfer of a site from
one Organization to another.

| Model            | Who owns the site                                                                                | When it fits                                                                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agency-owned** | Your Organization. You [share the site](#give-the-client-access) with the client's Organization. | You host and maintain the site as a service.                                           |
| **Client-owned** | The client's Organization. The client shares the site with your Organization.                    | The client wants to own the site, its connections and its subscription from the start. |

A shared site follows its **owner's** Cloud access and trial, not the
collaborating Organization's. If a client may take the site elsewhere later,
the [content export](../export-and-backup/) works for any owner.

## Publish each client to the right account

Your Organization connects GitHub, Cloudflare and media storage once under
**Account → Publishing**; see [Connect your accounts](../customer-publishing/).
Every site reuses those connections.

When a client's sites must publish to the **client's own Cloudflare account**,
add a **Hosting Group** for that account in the **Hosting Groups** tab, then
place the client's sites in it. Each Hosting Group owns one Cloudflare hosting
account and a site address base. GitHub, the build provider, DNS access and
shared media stay at the Organization level.

- Choose the Hosting Group before a site's first publication. A published site
  cannot simply switch groups; moving it is a hosting migration.
- Agents with an organization API key can manage groups with
  `list_hosting_groups`, `save_hosting_group`, `connect_hosting_group` and
  `set_site_hosting_group`.

## Give the client access

Give clients access to their site only, not to your whole Organization:

1. The client creates their own Typeroll Organization (signing up includes this
   step).
2. On the site, an admin opens **Site settings → Access & AI → Manage
   sharing** and shares the site with the client's Organization, choosing **read**, **write** (edit
   content, the default) or **admin**. Agents use `share_site` with the
   Organization's slug.
3. The client's people sign in to their own Organization and see the shared
   site there.

Change or revoke the share at any time with `update_site_share` or
`revoke_site_share`; revoking ends access immediately. A recipient cannot pass
the access on.

Inviting a client into **your** Organization instead gives them access to all
of its sites. Use invitations for your own team; see
[Teams and roles](../teams-and-roles/).

## Reviews and approvals

- **Previews:** share an expiring preview link of a page or a branch for
  feedback before anything is published (`get_preview_link`). See
  [Drafts, saving and review](../../tools/drafts-and-saving/).
- **Branches:** build a redesign on a branch and merge it only after the
  client's approval; see [Versions and branches](../../tools/versions/).
- **Owner answers:** when the client must confirm facts such as prices, opening
  hours or team members, use [Owner answers and review](../owner-review/). The
  client approves changes through a private link without signing in.

## Work across many sites with agents

- Create an **organization API key** under **Account → API keys**
  for an agent that manages several sites, or use a personal connection that
  has your own access. See [Connect your AI agent](../../getting-started/mcp-server/).
- Agents select one site at a time and confirm it before writing. Give each
  client its own [agent workspace](../../getting-started/agent-workspace/) for
  the brief, decisions and QA.
- Publish one site at a time per Organization: all sites share the
  Organization's build engine.
- `list_organization_analytics` and `set_organization_analytics` turn on the
  cookieless [Analytics module](../../apps/analytics/) for several sites at once.
- Run the [launch checklist](../launch-checklist/) on every new client site.

## When a client leaves

- **The client takes the site elsewhere:** download the
  [content export](../export-and-backup/) for them. The published static files
  already live in the Cloudflare account of the site's Hosting Group.
- **The site is retired:** [archive it](../archive-a-site/). Archiving keeps
  everything readable and the last publication online, and refuses changes.
- **The collaboration ends on a client-owned site:** the client revokes your
  Organization's share, which also ends access for your Organization's keys.

## Check it

- `list_site_shares` on each client site shows only the intended
  Organizations and permissions.
- `read_site_hosting_group` shows the client's Hosting Group before the first
  publication.
- `read_site_readiness` has no open warnings you have not accepted.
